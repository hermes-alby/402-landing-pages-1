## Research

## Summary
People searching for LinkedIn enrichment tools are primarily sales teams, recruiters, and growth engineers who need verified contact data (emails, phone numbers) from LinkedIn profiles. The market is dominated by subscription-based tools like Apollo (275M+ contacts), ZoomInfo (500M+ contacts), and Lusha — all requiring monthly commitments starting at $49-200+/month. Common frustrations include stale data (contacts showing old employers), credit systems that punish usage variability, and complex enterprise agreements for API access. There's growing interest in pay-per-result models (LeadMagic, Apify at $0.03/person) and AI-powered natural language search (Pearch AI, HeroHunt.ai, Data Legion) that eliminates boolean query complexity. The intersection of these two trends — pay-per-query pricing combined with natural language AI search — is an underserved niche that PayPerQ directly addresses.
